Dear Abi,

I don’t think I’ll ever be able to put into words just how much you mean to me, but I hope you always know that I choose you, even on the days when things aren’t easy. I know I’m not perfect and I know I’ve expected too much from you sometimes, but I’m trying to learn, to understand you better and to love you in a way that feels right for both of us. I never want you to feel like you have to be someone else for me or like you have to earn your place in my life, because you already have it. I love the little things about you, the way you make me laugh, the way you talk, your voice, your smile, even the parts of you that you probably don’t realise I notice. I just want us to keep growing together, meeting each other halfway, and choosing each other through all the messy, quiet, beautiful parts of life. No matter how loud life gets, I hope you can always come back to this and remember that there is someone here who loves you deeply, believes in you, and is endlessly grateful that I get to call you mine.
